Project Overview & Social Context

Studying how IT-based technological solutions contribute to addressing social and educational issues.

Addressing Social & Educational Challenges through IT

This website is developed as part of the SYBCA Field Work project under Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU). The field work investigates how IT, manufacturing, and telecommunications industries participate in building solutions that solve critical societal problems.

In the field of education, traditional manual attendance systems consume valuable instructional time and suffer from inaccuracies such as proxy attendance. By leveraging Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision, our proposed face-recognition attendance prototype demonstrates how technological innovation can streamline administrative tasks and improve institutional efficiency.

Key Features of Proposed System

Four primary capabilities designed to automate and modernise classroom attendance.

📷

Face Recognition

Captures biometric facial features using standard video cameras to identify enrolled students accurately.

⚡

Automated Attendance

Instantly marks student attendance in real-time as soon as their face is recognized, eliminating manual roll calls.

📊

Digital Records

Stores attendance data digitally in structured logs, replacing vulnerable paper registers with secure records.

📈

Attendance Analytics

Generates automated statistical reports, percentage calculations, and trend analysis for faculty and administration.
