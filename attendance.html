Dashboard Features Implemented

  • Real-time Search: Instantly filter student records by entering name characters or roll numbers.
  • Status Filter: Select dropdown views to isolate 'Present' or 'Absent' student lists.
  • Interactive Attendance Update: Click 'Mark Present' on any absent student to manually record their attendance timestamp.
  • Dynamic Statistics Engine: Present/Absent totals and overall Attendance Percentage automatically re-calculate upon any status change.
  • Instant Notification Toasts: Visual feedback popups confirm attendance record updates.
