About the Field Work Project

Academic background, study objectives, field activities, and SWOT analysis under Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU).

Academic Context & Curriculum

This website and technical study have been developed as part of the SYBCA (Second Year Bachelor of Computer Applications) Curriculum under Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU).

Course: SYBCA – SPPU Field Work

Topic: Industries (IT/Manufacturing/Telecomm) involved in development of solutions to solve social issues

Project Title: AI-Based Face Recognition Attendance System

Field Work Objectives

Five primary academic goals established for this field study.

1. Problem Analysis

To study attendance-management problems and inefficiencies in educational institutions.

2. IT Solution Assessment

To understand how IT-based technologies can address real-world social and educational problems.

3. Feasibility of AI Technology

To study the potential use of face recognition technology for automated attendance.

4. Methodological Evaluation

To identify operational limitations and challenges of existing manual attendance methods.

5. Technical Solution Proposal

To propose a practical, technology-based solution that improves institutional administration.

SWOT / SWOC Analysis

Evaluating internal and external factors affecting system implementation.

💪 Strengths

  • Fully automated attendance recording
  • Saves 10-15 minutes of lecture time
  • Creates instant digital records in memory
  • Eliminates paper register damage and loss

⚠️ Weaknesses

  • Camera hardware dependency
  • Potential recognition errors in dim lighting
  • Initial student registration setup required

🚀 Opportunities

  • Integration with AI attendance analytics
  • Mobile applications for faculty access
  • Cloud database synchronization across campuses

🛡️ Challenges / Threats

  • Student biometric data privacy concerns
  • Database security and encryption requirements
  • Spoofing risks using printed photo masks

Overall Field Work Conclusion

In conclusion, technology plays an increasingly pivotal role in solving social and operational issues in educational institutions. Implementing an AI-based face recognition attendance system dramatically improves institutional efficiency, reduces paper waste, and saves classroom teaching time, while requiring thoughtful consideration regarding biometric privacy, system security, and optical accuracy.
